Helpful Resources

Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom

This website is basically a textbook covering almost anything you might have questions about. It is well organized, making it easy to find exactly what you are looking for.

Hook Theory

Hook Theory is a database of over 75,000 analyzed songs. This is a great site to mess around on to see how classical music theory relates and differs from the popular music of today.

MusicTheory.net

Musictheory.net provides tons of practice exercises and lessons for many music theory concepts. They also have apps: Theory Lessons for their lessons and Tenuto for their practice exercises.

Teoria

Similar to MusicTheory.net, Teoria has lots of practice exercises and lessons, though Teoria has more options for ear training and sight singing.
