Where Is My Cat?

The Bed

If I'm ever looking for my cat, the first place to check is the bed in the spare bedroom. it's where we kept him when we first took him home as a kitten.

Near My Dog

My cat sometimes wants to cuddle with my dog. It lasts until my dog realizes he's there!

The Chair

My cat loves a good chair. He sometimes blends in!

Someone's arm

I'm not exactly sure why, but my cat like to lay on peoples hands and arms. It's not very comfortable, but he seems to like it!

The Chair (Part 2)

Seriously... he is often found on a chair somewhere in the house.

The Bathtub

You would think as a cat he would avoid a place so close to water, but for some reason my cat likes to lay in the bath tub. It's important to check if he's there before turning on the water!
